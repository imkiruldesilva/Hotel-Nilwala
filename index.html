About Hotel Nilwala

Welcome to Hotel Nilwala

Discover a peaceful escape at Hotel Nilwala, Aluthgama. Located along the beautiful river and just moments away from the beach, our hotel offers a relaxing atmosphere for travellers, families, couples and groups. Experience the beauty of Sri Lanka while enjoying warm hospitality and memorable moments.

Since opening our doors in 1992, we have welcomed guests from around the globe to our tranquil waterside property. Whether you are seeking a peaceful river safari, thrilling water sports, or a serene base to explore Sri Lanka's south-western coast, our dedicated team is here to make your stay exceptional.

Why Choose Hotel Nilwala?

An intimate boutique hotel where personalized Sri Lankan care meets the tranquil beauty of the waterways.

Established in 1992

Over three decades of welcoming international and local guests with trustworthy warmth, dedication, and genuine hospitality.

Beautiful River Views

Relax right beside the peaceful waters. Watch boats drift by and soak in the soothing natural atmosphere of Aluthgama.

Close to the Beach

Moments from Moragalla and Bentota golden beaches, giving you effortless access to both river serenity and ocean waves.

Comfortable Accommodation

Approximately 10 well-appointed boutique rooms with quiet air conditioning, private bathrooms, free Wi-Fi, and scenic balconies.

Water Sports Experiences

From thrilling jet-skiing and banana boats to wakeboarding, enjoy exciting watersports coordinated directly in the surrounding waters.

Boat Safari Arrangements

Hotel Nilwala arranges guided boat safaris through lush mangrove tunnels, spotting birds, river monitors, and tropical wildlife.

Wedding Celebrations

Host intimate riverfront wedding receptions, family milestones, and anniversary celebrations in an enchanting open-air setting.

Friendly Hospitality

Our dedicated family-driven team ensures attentive, respectful service that makes every guest feel truly at home in Sri Lanka.

Nature & Adventure

Experience the Beauty of the River

Discover the peaceful beauty of the surrounding waterways and enjoy memorable experiences with exciting river activities.

Boat Safari

Venture through tranquil mangrove sanctuaries, river islets, and observe indigenous bird species and river monitors. Boat safari arrangements can be organized through Hotel Nilwala.

River Exploration

Explore the scenic waterways of Aluthgama and Bentota where the freshwater meets the tropical coastal landscape. Experience local river life and pristine waters.

Water Sports

Adrenaline-fueled activities including jet skis, banana boats, tube rides, and water skiing arranged through trusted watersport partners along the Bentota river.

Relaxing River Experiences

Unwind at our open-air terrace with a refreshing tropical beverage while gentle river breezes soothe your senses. Peaceful dining right by the water.

Arranged Exclusively for Hotel Nilwala Guests

Boat safari arrangements can be organized smoothly through Hotel Nilwala. Let our team customize your morning or sunset river journey.
