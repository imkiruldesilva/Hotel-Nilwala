Nearby Attractions around Aluthgama & Bentota

All attraction photographs below are genuine Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons images depicting the exact authentic landmarks located just minutes from Hotel Nilwala.

Wikipedia Commons Verified Photo

Bentota Golden Beach

1.2 km • ~5 mins

World-renowned coastal beach where the Bentota River meets the Indian Ocean. Stretches of golden sands, tropical palm groves, swimming in turquoise waters, and breathtaking sunset panoramas.

Golden Sands Swimming Ocean Sunsets
Wikipedia Commons Verified Photo

Kande Viharaya Temple

2.5 km • ~8 mins

Historic Buddhist temple founded in 1734, situated atop a hill in Aluthgama. Houses one of the tallest seated Buddha statues in the world (approx. 48 meters high), sacred Bo-tree, and ancient relic chambers.

Cultural Heritage Historic Temple Giant Buddha Statue
Wikipedia Commons Verified Photo

Brief Garden by Bevis Bawa

8.5 km • ~20 mins

The legendary landscape garden and estate created by renowned Sri Lankan landscape artist Bevis Bawa. Winding jungle corridors, exotic ferns, serene reflection ponds, and distinctive eclectic art installations.

Botanical Garden Art & Architecture Tropical Greenery
Wikipedia Commons Verified Photo

Madu Ganga Mangrove Estuary

18 km • ~25 mins

A Ramsar-designated biodiversity wetland comprising over 60 natural mangrove islets. Highlights include Cinnamon Island peeling demonstrations, ancient island Buddhist temples, and fish therapy spas.

Ramsar Wetland Cinnamon Island Eco-Tourism
Wikipedia Commons Verified Photo

Kosgoda Turtle Sanctuary

12 km • ~18 mins

Dedicated marine conservation project safeguarding vulnerable sea turtle species (Green, Olive Ridley, Hawksbill, Leatherback). Observe hatcheries and learn about conservation efforts along the southern coastline.

Marine Life Turtle Hatchery Eco-Education
Wikipedia Commons Verified Photo

Barberyn Island Lighthouse

6 km • ~12 mins

Historic 1889 British colonial lighthouse towering 34 meters high on picturesque Barberyn Island off Beruwala. Accessible by a scenic short boat crossing, offering sweeping views over the southern sea.

Historic Lighthouse Offshore Island Ocean Panorama
